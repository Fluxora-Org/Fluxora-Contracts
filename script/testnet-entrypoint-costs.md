## Notes

- The **ratio** is testnet/local. The 2.0x figure sits at the midpoint of the
  documented 1.5–3x range; it reflects Wasm instantiation metering that the
  test host skips.
- The **ceiling** column records the larger of the two figures and is the
  number that integrators should budget against.
- Even the highest ceiling (`delegate_top_up` at ~2.3M instructions) is
  **less than 0.6% of the 400,000,000-instruction protocol limit**. Instructions
  are therefore not the binding resource constraint; the event byte budget is,
  and that is what `MAX_BATCH_SIZE` is derived from (see §3 of
  `docs/KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md`).
- Ledger entry counts and event bytes (the constraints that determine
  `MAX_BATCH_SIZE`) are *not* captured here; those are accurate in the local
  suite because they do not depend on execution mode.
- Re-run `script/measure-entrypoint-costs.sh` after any Soroban protocol
  upgrade, SDK version bump, or contract redeployment at a new address.

## Reconciliation with docs/KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md §2

The ceiling column above shows the measured upper bound from network simulation.
The local baseline (`contracts/stream/entrypoint-cost-baseline.json`) is not
replaced — it continues to gate CI regressions via `script/validate_gas.py`.
The testnet figures in `script/testnet-entrypoint-costs.json` document the
realistic production budget. The ceiling is the number integrators should
plan against.
